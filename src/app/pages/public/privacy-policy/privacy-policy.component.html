Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

This policy explains what information SalesDesk collects, why, and how it's handled. SalesDesk is a quotation and invoice management tool for freelancers and small businesses — this policy covers the SalesDesk web app and its Android/iOS apps alike.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text) when you register.
  • Workspace/business profile: your business name, tagline, address, logo, country, and default currency.
  • Customer and document data: the client names, contact details, quotes, invoices, and product/service records you create — this is your business data, which you control.
  • Payment information: if you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is processed by PayMongo (card, GCash, or other supported methods); SalesDesk never sees or stores your full card number. If you pay via manual GCash transfer, we retain the reference number and any screenshot you submit only to verify that specific payment.
  • Usage and device information: basic analytics (pages visited, features used) and, for the mobile app, a device push-notification token if you enable notifications.

How we use this information

  • To provide the core service — creating, sending, and tracking your quotes and invoices.
  • To verify your email address, reset your password, and secure your account.
  • To send transactional emails: document delivery, payment reminders, and account notices.
  • To process subscription payments and activate the plan you paid for.
  • To improve the product, using aggregated, non-identifying usage patterns.

Third-party services

SalesDesk relies on a small number of third-party processors to operate:

  • PayMongo — payment processing for paid subscriptions (Philippines).
  • Resend — transactional email delivery, where configured.
  • Railway / Vercel — hosting for the API and web app respectively.

None of these providers receive more data than they need to perform their specific function, and none are permitted to use your data for their own marketing purposes.

Data retention

Your account and business data are retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your workspace data is removed within a reasonable period, except where we're required to keep records (e.g. payment records) for legal or accounting purposes.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or export your business data at any time from within the app. You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the address below.

Security

Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to production systems is restricted to the operators of this service.

Children's privacy

SalesDesk is a business tool not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We'll update the "last updated" date above whenever this policy changes. Continued use of SalesDesk after a change means you accept the revised policy.
